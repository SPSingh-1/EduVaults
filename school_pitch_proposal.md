### 1. The Challenges in Modern School Management
* **Administrative Silos:** Separate tools for fees, grades, and timetables lead to duplicate work and errors.
* **Parent Communication Gaps:** Traditional notices are often lost, leading to delays in fee payments or academic updates.
* **Security & Backups:** Physical registers and local spreadsheets are vulnerable to loss, damage, and lack privacy controls.

### 2. The Solution: EduVault
EduVault consolidates all school activities into a single secure platform. Whether you are on desktop or mobile, the entire portal is responsive and easy to navigate.

| Feature Module | What It Does for Your School |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Student Directory** | Complete digital profile with dates of birth, parents' details, class logs, and history. |
| **Academics Section** | Structure courses, subjects, schedules, class sections, and class teacher allocations. |
| **Exam & Gradebook** | Create exams, input scores, request super admin approval, and release results to parents. |
| **Fees & Invoicing** | Setup structured fees, auto-generate invoices, accept card payments, and register transactions. |
| **Portals & Support** | Dedicated logins for Admins, Teachers, Students, and Parents. Direct support ticketing. |
